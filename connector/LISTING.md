# OnRankSEO — connector listing

**Name:** OnRankSEO
**URL:** https://onrankseo.com/api/mcp
**Transport:** streamable HTTP
**Authentication:** OAuth 2.1 with PKCE (S256) and dynamic client registration, against the user's existing OnRankSEO account. No API keys.
**Documentation:** https://onrankseo.com/mcp
**Contact:** hello@onrankseo.com

**Short description:** Real SEO audits for Claude: crawl a site against 38 audit checks, read findings with their evidence and the published rule each rests on, and draft a report a person signs.

**Long description:** OnRankSEO's tools run a real audit rather than look up a keyword database. Every finding comes back with what was seen, where and when, and the rule it rests on; every number with its source, date and method; anything unmeasured as "not measured — needs <KEY>". The tools draft and record; they never send a report or an email to a client — a person at OnRankSEO checks and signs every report. They never promise a ranking, a date, traffic or an AI citation, and never invent a score.

**Tools:** 22 (16 read-only). Write tools record findings, open audits, gather data and queue email drafts — only within what the signed-in account can do in the OnRankSEO console.
